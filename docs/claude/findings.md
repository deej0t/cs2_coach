## Findings, Baselines, Relevance (`findings.py`)
- **All statistics live in `evidence.py`**: `compare_groups()` (means, Cohen's d) and `compare_rates()` (proportions, Cohen's h), both returning a `Comparison` with CI and verdict. `findings.py` re-exports the old private names (`_effect_ci` etc.) for compatibility. Pass `comparisons=k` when the group was picked as the best/worst of k candidates (Bonferroni) — picking the best of seven weekdays always finds one.
- **No page may give advice from a raw mean difference with a fixed threshold.** Tilt, sessions, pistol, momentum and calendar all did ("Tilt-Effekt erkannt" at delta > 0.08, "bester Tag" from 5 matches, "schlechteste Uhrzeit" from 2, "Pause nach dem 3. Match" from 2 late games). They now state the measurement and only advise when the comparison is solid. "No difference measured" is not "stable" — that needs `no_difference`.
- `/momentum` takes its tilt verdict from `_build_tilt_analysis()`; it used to compute its own with fixed thresholds that could contradict `/tilt`.
- Findings are derived from the already-exported player JSON, not from the report prose — so they apply retroactively to all exports without re-parsing a demo. They are deliberately **not** persisted: thresholds live in one place and changes take effect across the whole history.
- `training_priorities()` is the **single source** for training goals on both the dashboard and `/coaching`. It excludes outcome-driven metrics (survival, K/D, ADR, KAST — they rise simply by winning the round) and rules without discriminatory power.
- `build_baselines()` computes the user's personal p10–p90 distribution and flags rules that fire in >90% or <5% of matches as non-discriminating.
- `build_relevance()` reports Cohen's d **with a 95% confidence interval** and a verdict (robustly positive/negative, proven irrelevant, undecided). Never present an effect size as fact — at ~58 matches most are undecided. For undecided cases it also computes how many matches would settle the question.
- Windows are aggregated by **median**, not mean; one 21-0 match otherwise fakes a collapse.
- **Match order: sort export files with `obsidian.export_sort_key`, never by plain filename.** Names are `<date>_<map>_<score>_<HHMM>_coach.json`, so a plain sort orders a day by map and score: 35 of 62 consecutive pairs were wrong, and the shown "slight tilt effect" (delta 0.06) came entirely from that (correct order: -0.01). `_get_exports()` returns **newest first** — anything that walks a session forward must re-sort ascending (`_build_sessions` had tilt and warm-up swapped for 8 sessions).
- Tilt compares only the next match **in the same session** (same date, like `/sessions`); the match three days later says nothing about tilt. Draws count as neither win nor loss.
- `utility_analysis.throw_relevance()` breaks utility down **by grenade type, per round** (not per match — a 13:5 has 18 rounds, a 13:11 has 24, so per-match partly measures round count). It uses `evidence.compare_groups()` like everything else; never reimplement the statistics.
- **Measured 2026-10-14 over 93 matches** (server): per-round throws - total d=0.94 [0.51…1.37], HE 0.85 [0.42…1.28] and molotovs 0.83 [0.41…1.25] robustly positive; flashes 0.48 and smokes 0.44 undecided. **The 2026-09-21 claim "smokes are flat (d=0.12), your smokes are reliable" no longer holds** - with more data smokes point the same way. Always re-read the verdict instead of quoting an older number; verdicts move as the sample grows (utility total went 0.69 undecided at 58 → 0.77 robust at 62 → 0.94 at 93).
- **Economy does not explain the utility effect** (measured 2026-11-12, 131 matches, full-buy rounds only via `current_equip_value` at freeze end >= `_FORCE_THRESHOLD`): grenades per full-buy round 2.35 in wins vs 1.81 in losses, d = 0.84 [0.48...1.20] robust; HE 0.69 and molotovs 0.66 robust, smokes 0.38 / flashes 0.51 undecided. Round level (full buy, alive at 35 s): a grenade in the first 35 s -> 63 % rounds won (1,059) vs 55 % without (355), h = 0.17 [0.05...0.29] - positive but small. Script was ad hoc (`/tmp/eco_util.py`), not in the repo. Live coach (GSI) ran in 17 matches from 2026-11-03: utility/round 2.05 with vs 1.99 without (d = 0.10, undecided); it counts ~0.5 grenades per round taken to the grave.
- **Own vs team vs enemy utility** (2026-11-13, 131 matches, scoreboard `utility_per_round` of all 10, teams via schema-5 `team`): you 1.95 vs 1.41/round (d = +1.02 robust), teammates' mean 1.38 vs 1.17 (+0.61 robust), **enemies' mean 1.05 vs 1.44 (-0.93 robust)**. Logistic regression win ~ you + mates + enemies (per SD): you b = +1.50 [+0.82...+2.18], mates +0.79 [+0.26...+1.31], enemies -1.35 [-1.91...-0.78]; r(you, mates) = 0.25. So your own grenades are not just team mood - but the enemies throwing *fewer* in your wins shows match-level grenade counts are largely a *consequence* of winning rounds (alive longer, more money), for both teams. The cleaner causal size is the round-level one above (grenade in the first 35 s of a full buy: 63 vs 55 % rounds won, h = 0.17). Real, but much smaller than d ~ 1 suggests - do not sell the match-level d as the size of the lever.
- **"Taken to the grave" is no separate lever** (death_review v2 `nades`, measured 2026-11-12, 1,881 deaths): 1.06 grenades per death, 55 % of deaths with >= 1. Per death you carry *more* in wins (1.18 vs 0.95, d = -0.59 robust) - richer rounds, more bought; rounds after a death with grenades are won more often (31 vs 24 %). Share thrown = thrown / (thrown + grave) is 73 vs 64 % (d = 0.69 robust), but partly outcome-driven (early deaths in lost rounds keep fresh grenades). So no extra training goal - the HE/molotov goals already carry it.
- **Trades do not separate wins from losses either** (`death_review.py`, measured 2026-11-01 over 120 matches / 1,674 deaths): traded within 5 s 18.4 % in wins vs 16.4 % in losses (h = 0.05 [-0.05...0.15], robustly negligible); opening deaths 7.4 vs 9.0 % (robustly negligible); nearest teammate > 1000 units 37.9 vs 43.8 % (h = -0.12 [-0.22...-0.02], undecided). Only 17 % of all deaths are traded, median teammate distance 619 units. A round after a traded death is won 50 %, after an untraded one 22 % - outcome-driven (trades happen when the team is there), not a lever. So no "trade training" from this - the measured lever stays utility.
- **HE quality does not separate wins from losses - quantity does.** Damage per HE 8.25 vs 7.62 (d=0.10, undecided), HE damage per round 3.72 vs 2.56 (0.43, undecided, driven by count), throw second ~31 s in both. So the advice is "throw more HE", not "hit with your HE". Absolute level: 718 HE, 6,130 damage = 8.5 per HE; in 9 of 94 matches the HE did no damage at all - most HE hit nobody.
- `utility_analysis.he_effectiveness()` asks *how well*, not *how many*: damage per HE, HE damage per round, fire damage per round, median throw second. If damage per HE is robustly higher in wins, the advice is "hit with your HE", not "throw more". Matches without schema 4 are skipped, never counted as zero damage. For the throw second there is no better/worse - positive only means later in wins.
- **`Rule(observe_only=True)`** = a new metric without a verdict: it appears in `build_relevance` (/coaching) and `build_baselines`, but creates no finding, no track and no training goal, and `evaluate()` returns it only with `include_observed=True`. Without that, report_ai would pick it as "largest regression" = FOKUS (and its `LOWER_BETTER` set did not know it - wrong direction). Give it real thresholds only once the verdict is solid.
- **Hebel-Suche** (`levers.py`, 2026-11-16): the ad hoc measurements (team utility, zones, Leetify metrics, trades) used to end in "re-run with more matches" notes nobody re-ran. Now they run on every sync (0.6 s over 143 matches + 1,881 deaths). **Match-level grenade counts are flagged outcome-driven there** (`COUNT_KEYS` + `throw_relevance` rows): the enemy throws fewer in your wins (13.11.), so "Utility pro Runde belastbar" is no new lever. First run on 2026-11-16: no non-outcome candidate solid; early grenade per round h = +0.16 [+0.05...+0.27] (positive, below the 0.2 bar), 0 of 105 zones solid. Feeding a new solid lever into the briefing/mission automatically is not built yet - it needs a lever that exists first.
- **Win probability per round state** (`winprob.py`, 2026-11-16, local: 79 5v5 demos, 1,619 rounds, 15,339 cell visits; two Wingman demos drop out; scan 87 s, then cached). Why: wins-vs-losses can never see what you do the same in every match, and the match result colours every metric - a state value prices a single death regardless of how the round ended, and the other nine players of each demo are the reference. Out-of-sample (20 folds of whole matches): Brier 0.251 (CT rate only) -> 0.162 (players alive + bomb) -> 0.162 (+ time bucket) -> **0.151 (+ equipment)**; predicted vs observed deviates 0.7 points over ten bins. **Time adds almost nothing once the player counts are known. Equipment is not optional**: without it the table is right on average but 6.9 points off per equipment class (CT 2000 $/head behind: 38 % predicted, 25 % observed; 1.1 points with it), so every eco death would look dearer than it is. The slope falls almost linearly with players alive (0.36 at ten, 0.15 at four), hence one number: logit shift = 0.39 x (edge in 1000 $ - 0.39) x alive/10. The neutral point is needed - without it CT came out 1.4 points too high. Equipment is the freeze-end value, not what is still alive.
- **First cut, exploratory (ad hoc script, not in the repo): you vs the other nine of the same match, per match (n = 79), Bonferroni over 4.** Kill gain per round 14.3 vs 11.3 points (d = +0.78 [+0.37...+1.19], solid), death cost per round 11.0 vs 11.8 (d = +0.31 [-0.09...+0.71], undecided), deaths per round 0.68 vs 0.73 (undecided), cost per death 16.3 vs 16.1 (undecided). So **no sign that you die more expensively than the lobby overall** - the lever, if there is one, sits in a narrower cut.
- **Narrower cuts, measured 2026-11-19 (`duel_value.py`, same 79 demos, 11,669 deaths, all with callout; price per match from the out-of-fold table).** A duel = +price of the enemy when you win it, -your price when you lose it; reference = **the lobby at the same spot**, not your other zones (zones.py's blind spot: a zone you are always weak in can never stand out there). Per match: net per round 3.3 vs -0.5 (d = +0.84 [+0.44...+1.24], solid). **12 situations (side x lead x bomb): 0 solid**, the old "T even numbers" hint disappears - you win 57 vs 47 % of those duels, the dearer deaths come from more duels at 5v5. **52 zones with >= 15 duels each: 0 solid** (Bonferroni over 52). Weakest by effect, all undecided: Ancient CT BombsiteA 3 of 15 won vs 59 % (d = -0.79 [-1.70...+0.12]), Nuke CT Mini 6 of 20 (-0.41). So no zone lever yet - with the server's ~143 matches Ancient CT A is the one to re-check. Duels within a match are correlated; the Bonferroni over all zones is the guard. **In levers.py since 2026-11-20**: levels `Lage` and `Lobby-Zone` (all 12 situations as rows, zones only when solid; net per round and the five weakest zones as info card on /hebel). The sync reads at most `SCAN_PER_SYNC` = 60 new demos into winprob_cache/ (~1 s each locally, **~3 s on the server**: 144 demos in 435 s on 2026-11-20, cache filled by hand that day), the page never scans. **Lobby candidates have their own baseline** (`lobby_baseline` in .levers_state.json, set on the first run with nothing loaded and nothing waiting) - otherwise a verdict that only flips because old demos were loaded in batches would be reported as new. Messages say "du schwächer/besser als die Lobby", not "in Siegen". `matches_needed` is zeroed for these rows: it would count duels, without Bonferroni (17,300 for Mirage T B). A solid *positive* lobby zone is a strength, not homework - it is still reported, as for the other levels.
- **Server, 2026-11-20 (144 demos, 142 matches with you):** net per round 3.15 vs -0.45 (d = +0.89 [+0.65...+1.13]); 106 zones tested, **0 solid**. Weakest still Ancient CT BombsiteA, 7 of 24 duels won vs 111 of 196 for the lobby (d = -0.58 [-1.34...+0.18]), then Mirage CT Underpass, Cache T Outside A, Nuke CT Mini. Situations: all undecided, most slightly positive (you better than the lobby, lower bound above 0 but below the 0.2 bar).
- **Habits vs the lobby** (`duel_value.by_habit`, winprob cache v3, 2026-11-20, local 79 demos): per death the scan now reads the distance to the nearest *living* mate (2D, None = last man), grenades held (inventory one tick before) and own detonations in that round up to 2 s after the death (flight time). Rate = rounds with such a death / rounds on that side, you vs everyone else in the same demos (mates and enemies, so winning and losing sides alike), Bonferroni over 8, sign flipped so positive = it happens to you less. Result: **no habit where you are worse.** CT alone 21.6 vs 21.7 % and CT unused grenade 8.4 vs 10.2 % are *solid negligible*; early (before 25 s), opening and T unused grenade happen to you less (e.g. T opening 5.1 vs 11.0 %, h = +0.22 [+0.12...+0.32]) - lower bound > 0 but below the 0.2 bar, so still undecided. Last-man deaths never count as alone (otherwise surviving to the end would look like a weakness). Role confounds opening/early: an entry dies first by design. Rescan cost: local 175 s for 81 demos (v2: 92 s); **server 672 s for 144 demos (~4.7 s each, v2 ~3 s)**, filled by hand on 2026-11-20. Server result (142 matches with you): CT alone 19.3 vs 22.2 %, CT unused grenade 7.3 vs 9.9 %, T alone 10.3 vs 13.5 % - all *solid negligible*; early, opening and T unused grenade happen to you less (T opening 5.0 vs 11.2 %, h = +0.23 [+0.15...+0.31]; T early 12.0 vs 19.9 %, +0.22 [+0.14...+0.30]; T unused 7.2 vs 13.3 %, +0.20 [+0.12...+0.28]) - lower bounds 0.08-0.15, still below the 0.2 bar.
- **Lobby baseline is tied to the cache version** (`lobby_baseline` = winprob.CACHE_KEY): a new cache key means all demos are re-read in batches, and lobby levels stay silent until a run with nothing loaded and nothing waiting. A v2-era cache event with fewer fields is padded by position (an earlier `+ ["", "", None...]` padding turned the mate distance into "" and crashed the habit test).
- **Costliest deaths** (`costly_moments.py`, 2026-11-20) are a *fact per event*, not advice from a mean - they need no solid lever. Price distribution of your deaths (local, 1,100): last man alive median 6 but p90 50 and max 66 points, 2-5 alive median 15-19, max 28-46. Ranked raw, the top 8 were all lost 1v1 clutches, so only deaths with a mate alive are ranked. The review is told to present the dearest one as a scene to watch, never as a verdict (a dear death can be the right play).
- The effect-size table lives in `_partials/relevance_table.html` and is used by `/coaching` and `/utility`. Keep it shared — a page that drops the confidence interval reads like a statement of fact.
